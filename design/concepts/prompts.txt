=== a-control-room ===
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: high fidelity desktop application screen concept, 1600 by 1000 landscape aspect ratio.
Primary request: Design a polished, production-quality Korean desktop application for Studio Baekho, a game-development automation studio. This is direction A, "Control Room": an elegant operational workspace focused on seeing the day's queue and inspecting one selected task.
Composition: perfectly flat, straight-on full application screen, edge to edge, no presentation canvas, no device, no perspective. A compact 200px left sidebar, a wide central workspace, and a 330px task detail panel on the right. Consistent 8px spacing and clear typography at readable realistic desktop scale.
Palette: charcoal #14171C workspace, slightly raised graphite panels, warm white text, restrained amber #E9AF4A highlights, muted slate secondary text, thin subtle borders. Sophisticated and calm, no neon or gradients.
Brand at sidebar top exact: "BAEKHO / STUDIO". Small precise white tiger emblem. Project selector "사인검". Left nav exact labels: "오늘의 작업", "도구", "에셋", "파이프라인", "실행 기록"; bottom "설정". Current item "오늘의 작업" gets an amber-tinted background.
Main header "오늘의 작업", smaller caption "작업의 흐름을 한눈에 확인하세요". Header right quiet search icon, notification icon, circular avatar.
Beneath header, three compact summary counters in a single horizontal strip: "진행 중  3", "검토 대기  2", "완료  12". They must be modest; this is not an analytics dashboard.
A low-height elegant studio banner includes a tasteful monochrome ink-style white tiger walking through mist on only the right half, overall subtle and integrated into product UI, with left text "사인검" and "오늘도, 한 걸음 더." It occupies under 15 percent of the screen.
Central workspace section title "실행 대기열", tabs "전체", "진행 중", "검토 대기", a compact amber primary button "새 작업". Readable professional table, columns "작업", "상태", "진행", "업데이트". Five generously spaced rows with small distinct tool glyphs: "타격감 찾기 자동화" with amber "실행 중" and thin 68% progress bar, "모델링 자동화" with green "완료", "타격감 후보 비교" with slate "대기", "모델링 결과 검토" with green "완료", "타격감 결과 검토" with purple "검토 대기". First row selected subtly. No graphs.
Right detail panel: "작업 상세", selected title "타격감 찾기 자동화", restrained amber "실행 중" pill, metadata "프로젝트  사인검", "실행 도구  후보 탐색", "시작  14:32"; simple thin progress bar at 68%; section "최근 로그" with 3 short timestamped lines. Bottom secondary outline action "로그 열기" and a compact subdued pause control.
Tiny footer exact "예시 데이터 · 디자인 시안".
Constraints: mature professional desktop product, excellent hierarchy and contrast, short accurately rendered Korean labels, typography resembling a clean Korean neo-grotesk. Useful and believable task-centric UI. White tiger art is a supporting brand detail, never dominant. No browser chrome, no giant dashboard charts, no huge hero, no glowing neon, no gibberish ornamental text.
Product accuracy: the actual two core module names are "타격감 찾기 자동화" (finding and comparing tactile combat/impact feedback candidates with video-preview cards) and "모델링 자동화" (3D model preview and result version selection). All metrics and status are example data, and this is a conceptual UI direction, not a claim of implemented backend capability.
